Fruit Blasters

Playing as a rocketship, save the Earth from enemy fruit by shooting them and answer 5 multiple-choice questions that test your knowledge on cybersafety practices

In a world dominated by the internet, you need to know how to be safe online. But where can you learn how to be safe online and have fun?
Fruit Blasters is the answer to this problem. In Fruit Blasters, you play as a rocketship, trying to save the Earth from incoming missiles shaped as fruit. You can dodge incoming fruit and shoot them to gain points. Try to survive while shooting fruit to get a score of 30 without losing all 3 lives.
Once you earn a score of 30, you have to complete an online safety quiz that tests your knowledge on online safety practices. If you get all 5 questions correct, you save the Earth.
This simple and fun game can drain hours of your time and teach you important online skills that will help you be safe online now and in the future. Don't miss out on this brilliant game for only $39.99.
